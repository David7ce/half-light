## Tagline

## Short description (itch "short description" field)

A short puzzle platformer about being one person in two worlds at once.

## Description

Half-Light is a small puzzle platformer made for **Game Off 2026**, whose theme
is **SHIFT**.

You are one person with two states. Press Shift and you cross over:

- **The living** walk on furniture, but a wall is a wall.
- **The dead** walk through walls, but they fall through furniture — and salt
  burns them.

Every room is built so that neither state gets through alone. Four rooms, one
rule, and a door that only opens for the living, with every echo in hand.

The game is short on purpose: one mechanic, learned in the first room and asked
for by the last.

## Controls

- `←` `→` or `A` `D` — walk
- `Space`, `↑` or `W` — jump
- `Shift` — cross over
- `R` — restart the room
- `M` — mute

Touch controls appear on phones and tablets.

## Theme

"SHIFT" is the whole game: the shift key is the only verb that is not walking.
The room design takes the theme literally — shifting is how you change which
laws of the house apply to you.
